    How the chair gets booked.

    There's one barber and one appointment book. Here's how to get your name in it.

    1. Pick your cut.

      On the price board above, or straight in Timely. Bringing a mate or the kids? Duo and Hat-Trick slots book two or three cuts back to back.

    2. Choose a time.

      Appointments run Monday to Friday, 10 am to 6 pm. You can book online any hour.

    3. Hold it with a card.

      A card on file confirms the booking. Full card details aren't stored, and nothing is charged unless you don't show or cancel outside the required time.

    4. Be on time.

      The book is busy. Arrive late and your cut may not fit, with the full fee still charged.
